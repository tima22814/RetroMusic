Retro Music is a simple offline music player for the songs stored on your phone, styled after the glossy music players of the mid-2000s.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Library by artists, albums and songs with a quick A–Z index</li>
<li>Cover Flow album carousel when the phone is turned sideways</li>
<li>Now Playing screen with the queue on the back of the cover</li>
<li>Shuffle, background playback, notification and lock screen controls</li>
<li>Hide albums or folders from the library without deleting files</li>
<li>Light (classic) and dark themes</li>
</ul>

No internet access, no ads, no tracking.
